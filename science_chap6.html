⚗️ Chapter 6: Acids and Alkalis

Explore the characteristics of acids and alkalis, use indicators to measure pH, and understand neutralization.

🧪 6.1 Properties of Acids and Alkalis

Acids and alkalis show specific chemical properties only in the presence of water.

  • Acids:
    - Have a sour taste.
    - pH value is less than 7 (pH less than 7).
    - Turns moist blue litmus paper red.
    - Reacts with metals to produce hydrogen gas (e.g., hydrochloric acid, vinegar, citrus fruits).
  • Alkalis:
    - Have a bitter taste and feel soapy.
    - pH value is more than 7 (pH greater than 7).
    - Turns moist red litmus paper blue (e.g., soap, toothpaste, bleach).

⚖️ 6.2 Neutralization

Neutralization is a reaction when an acid reacts with an alkali to produce salt and water only.

  • Word Equation:
    $$\text{Acid} + \text{Alkali} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Water}$$
  • Applications in Daily Life:
    - Toothpaste: Alkaline toothpaste neutralizes acids produced by bacteria in the mouth to prevent tooth decay.
    - Antacids: Consumed to neutralize excess stomach acid for gastric relief.
    - Agriculture: Slaked lime (alkali) is added to acidic soil to adjust pH levels for crops.

📊 6.3 Indicators and the pH Scale

An indicator is a chemical substance that changes color depending on whether it is in an acid or an alkali.

  • Litmus Paper: Turns blue to red (in acid) and red to blue (in alkali).
  • Universal Indicator: Shows different colors across the pH scale (0 to 14).
    - pH 0 - 6: Acidic (Red, Orange, Yellow).
    - pH 7: Neutral (Green - pure water).
    - pH 8 - 14: Alkaline (Blue, Purple).
  • Phenolphthalein: Colourless in acid, pink in alkali.

🏭 6.4 Uses in Industry and Agriculture

Both substances play vital roles in manufacturing and science laboratories.

  • Sulphuric Acid: Used in car batteries, making fertilizers, and detergents.
  • Hydrochloric Acid: Used in metal cleaning processes and swimming pool maintenance.
  • Sodium Hydroxide / Ammonia: Used in manufacturing soap, glass, and household cleaners.
