🌿 Chapter 2: Ecosystem

Explore how energy flows, nutrients cycle, and how organisms interact with each other and their environment.

☀️ 2.1 Energy Flow in Ecosystems

Energy transfers from one organism to another through feeding relationships.

  • Producers: Organisms that make their own food through photosynthesis (e.g., green plants).
  • Consumers: Organisms that eat other organisms.
    - Primary: Herbivores (eat plants).
    - Secondary: Carnivores/Omnivores (eat primary consumers).
    - Tertiary: Apex predators (eat secondary consumers).
  • Decomposers: Microorganisms (bacteria, fungi) that break down dead organisms into simpler substances.
  • Food Web: A network of interconnected food chains in an ecosystem.

♻️ 2.2 Nutrient Cycle

Nutrients are continuously transferred through the living and non-living environment.

  • Water Cycle: Driven by evaporation (from water bodies), transpiration (from plants), condensation, and precipitation (rain).
  • Carbon Cycle: Carbon dioxide is absorbed by plants during photosynthesis and released back into the air through respiration, combustion, and decay.
  • Oxygen Cycle: Oxygen is produced by plants during photosynthesis and used by living things for respiration and combustion.

🤝 2.3 Interactions Among Organisms

Organisms in a community rely on each other to survive.

  • Symbiosis: Close relationship between two different species.
    - Mutualism: Both benefit (e.g., sea anemone and clownfish).
    - Commensalism: One benefits, the other is unharmed (e.g., remora fish and shark).
    - Parasitism: One benefits, the host is harmed (e.g., tapeworm in a human).
  • Prey-Predator: A predator hunts and eats the prey (e.g., tiger and deer).
  • Competition: Organisms compete for limited resources like food, water, or light.

🌳 2.4 Human's Role in Maintaining Balance

Human activities heavily impact the balance of nature. We must take action to preserve it.

  • Negative Impacts: Deforestation, industrial pollution, overfishing, and burning of fossil fuels disrupt the ecosystem.
  • Solutions to Maintain Balance:
    - Practice 3R (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle).
    - Enforce laws against illegal logging and poaching.
    - Reforestation (replanting trees) and creating national parks/reserves.
